# Contacts and contracts

This chapter tells how Presswork keeps the data of persons and organizations outside of the team, and the agreements with them.

## Contacts

- The contacts directory keeps authors, translators, illustrators, freelancers, reviewers, printers, distributors, and rights holders.
- A contact is a person or an organization. A person can have an ORCID identifier.
- A contact can also have a Presswork account, for example a guest account for the web portal.
- Each contact shows its links: tasks, contracts, clearances, submissions, time entries, publications, and messages. Refer to [Connections](connections.md).
- Contact data is personal data. Only persons with the `contact.read` permission can see it. The AI gateway removes it from AI requests.

## Contracts

- A contract records an agreement with a contact: the type, the dates, the publication, the terms, and the fees.
- A contract has deliverables, for example "Manuscript", "Corrected proofs", or "Index". Each deliverable becomes a task or a milestone with its due date.
- A contract keeps the agreement document as a file.
- Presswork shows a warning before a deliverable is late and before a contract expires.
- The fees of a contract become cost items in the budget. Refer to [Tasks and schedules](schedules.md).

Royalty statements are not in the plan.
